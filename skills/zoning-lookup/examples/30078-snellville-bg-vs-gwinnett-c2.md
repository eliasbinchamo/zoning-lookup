# Zoning Summary: General Commercial in ZIP 30078 — City of Snellville (BG) vs. Unincorporated Gwinnett County (C-2), GA

**Site:** ZIP 30078 only (no parcel given). The ZIP is entirely in Gwinnett County. About **53%** of its land is inside the **City of Snellville**; the other **47%** is **unincorporated Gwinnett County** (Census 2020 ZCTA–place relationship file). Each has its own zoning code, so which one applies depends on the parcel.
**Districts compared:** Snellville **BG General Business** and Gwinnett **C-2 General Business**. These are each jurisdiction's general commercial district. The other commercial options are listed at the end.
**Ordinances:**
- **Snellville:** Unified Development Ordinance (UDO), Appendix A of the City Code, adopted 10-26-2020. Municode **Supplement 33, Update 2, online 9-11-2026**.
- **Gwinnett County:** Unified Development Ordinance, **Title 2 Land Use and Zoning, amended August 2026** (official county PDF).
**Prepared:** 2026-11-11. This is a research summary, not legal advice.

## Bottom line

The two codes take opposite approaches:
- **Snellville BG** allows **taller buildings (80 ft)** and **more lot coverage (90%)**, but sets **real setbacks** (10 ft front and sides, 20 ft rear). Parking is a minimum only, generally **1 space per 500 sf**, and restaurants need more.
- **Gwinnett C-2** has **zero setbacks**, but caps height at **45 ft**, coverage at **80%** and **FAR at 1.0**. Its buffers next to residential are much wider: **75 ft** next to single-family, versus 40 ft in Snellville. Parking has **both a minimum and a maximum**, e.g. retail 1/500 to 1/300 sf.

Uses differ in ways that matter:

| Use | Snellville BG | Gwinnett C-2 |
|---|---|---|
| Bars, nightclubs, taverns, lounges | Prohibited | Lounges/nightclubs by special use |
| Self-storage | Prohibited | Special use with conditions |
| Smoke and vape shops | Prohibited | Smoke/CBD shops by right |
| Hotels | City Council special use (2-acre minimum lot, at least 60 rooms) | Conditional use (interior corridors, 24-hour management) |
| Gas stations, car washes, light auto repair | Special approval needed | Special approval needed |

**Confirm which side of the city limit the parcel is on before relying on either set of numbers.**

## City of Snellville — BG General Business District

### Dimensional standards (Sec. 203-2.7 – 203-2.9)

| Standard | BG |
|---|---|
| Lot area / width | No minimum |
| Lot coverage | 90% max |
| Front / side (interior) / side (street) / rear setback | 10 / 10 / 10 / 20 ft min |
| Building height | 80 ft max |
| Building types | None required |
| Architecture | Sec. 201-3 General Architectural Standards |
| Open space | See Sec. 401-2 |

**Buffers (Sec. 207-2.1, Table 207-2.1.B):**
- A new BG project needs a **40 ft** buffer next to any residential district. That includes residential-zoned land in **unincorporated Gwinnett** (table note [2]).
- If a public right-of-way 60 ft wide or less separates the site from a residential district, the buffer is **20 ft and undisturbed**.
- The Board of Appeals can halve the buffer width if an opaque 8 ft wall is built. City Council can change buffers in a rezoning or special use approval.
- An abutting owner can consent in writing to reduce or eliminate a buffer only where that neighboring site is designated commercial, office-institutional or light manufacturing on the future land use plan.

**Open space (Sec. 401-2.1):** commercial, industrial and mixed-use developments **over 10 acres** must set aside **20%** of the site as open space. This does not apply in the TCO, TC, MU, NR or RO districts.

### Permitted uses (Sec. 206-2 Allowed Use Table, BG column)

**Legend:**
- **P** = permitted by right.
- **L** = Limited Use: permitted, subject to the use standard cited in the table.
- **S** = Special Use: requires City Council approval under Sec. 103-10.
- **—** = not permitted.

**By right (P):**
- **Retail:** general, grocery, department, drug, hardware, home-improvement and furniture stores; convenience food; CBD store; bottle/growler shop; guns and ammunition; plant nursery; auto parts with no installation; and more.
- **Office:** all office types, banks, finance company, trade and vocational schools.
- **Medical:** medical and dental offices, clinics, urgent care, emergency medical office, dialysis.
- **Personal services:** salons, dry cleaning, laundry, funeral home without crematorium, tutoring.
- **Animal care:** indoor and outdoor, including boarding, veterinary and kennel (indoor).
- **Indoor recreation:** gym and fitness, arcade, bowling, skating, indoor sports, indoor shooting range.
- **Day care:** adult care center, day care center.
- **Group care:** assisted living, nursing home, skilled nursing.
- **Production:** brewery, distillery, winery.
- **Civic:** community center, museum/library, public buildings, parks.
- **Transportation:** passenger terminals (bus, taxi, limousine, with no on-site vehicle storage).

**Limited (L), allowed subject to use standards:**
- **Restaurants:** all types, including drive-thru, take-out, coffee, ice cream and brewpub. The standards cover exhaust-fan placement and drive-thru rules (Sec. 206-8.9).
- **Retail:** package store (distilled spirits), loan office, meat market, consumer fireworks, artisan shop.
- **Medical:** medical cannabis dispensary, mobile health screening.
- **Other:** massage therapy, craft manufacturing, internet vehicle sales.
- **Accessory:** EV charging, drive-thru facility, minor outdoor storage.

**Special use (S), City Council approval:**
- **Lodging:** hotels, motels and extended stay. Minimum **2-acre** lot and at least **60 guest rooms** (Sec. 206-5.4).
- **Vehicle:** gas station with convenience store, car wash, minor vehicle repair (quick lube, glass, emissions), vehicle sales and rental, remote parking.
- **Indoor entertainment and events:** indoor event facility, theaters, assembly hall, convention center.
- **Outdoor recreation and storage:** all outdoor recreation, building supply with outdoor storage.
- **Retail and services:** check cashing, pawn and title pawn, tattoo and body piercing, fortune teller, funeral home with crematorium.
- **Institutional:** place of worship, schools, hospice, behavioral health facility, telecom tower, utility substation.

**Not permitted (—):**
- **Bars and nightlife:** bar, nightclub, tavern, lounge, hookah bar.
- **Shops:** smoke shop, vape shop.
- **Vehicle:** drive-in restaurant, **major vehicle repair** (body, brakes, tires, transmission), boat/RV sales.
- **Storage and industrial:** **self-storage and mini-warehouses**, warehousing and distribution, wholesale, contractor storage, light and heavy manufacturing, research and development labs.
- **Medical:** hospital, ambulatory surgical center, medical and dental laboratory.
- **Residential:** all household living, including **multifamily, townhomes and live-work**; personal care home.

### Parking (Sec. 207-1, Table 207-1.2.B; amended UDO 22-01, 2-28-2022 and UDO 25-01, 3-10-2025)

| Use | Minimum |
|---|---|
| All retail; all office; all personal services; most medical | 1 / 500 sf |
| Restaurant, freestanding | 1 / 150 sf |
| Restaurant, not freestanding | 1 / 300 sf |
| Hotel / motel / extended stay | 1 / guest room, plus restaurant or event space |
| Indoor recreation; event facility | 1 / 3 fixed seats + 1 / 200 sf without fixed seats |
| Hospital, urgent care, emergency medical office | 3 / bed |
| Day care center | 1 / 400 sf |
| Automobile parts store | 1 / 750 sf |
| Gas station | 1 / 500 sf |
| Car wash, full-service | 1 / 500 sf + 4 stacking spaces per bay |
| Vehicle repair | 1 / grease rack or similar |

- **Shopping centers** use their own ratio: **1 / 500 sf** under 15,000 sf and **1 / 1,000 sf** at 15,000 sf or more.
- **Small businesses:** establishments under **3,000 sf** need no parking when on a lot with another use, unless they are restaurants.
- **Outdoor dining** counts toward parking when it exceeds 25% of the restaurant's floor area.
- **Shared parking** reductions are allowed case by case using the ULI Shared Parking Model (Sec. 207-1.3).
- **No parking maximum** was found in Article 7.

### Overlay to check: Towne Center Overlay (Sec. 205-1)

Some BG parcels lie inside the **Towne Center Overlay**, which covers the city's downtown redevelopment area. Where the overlay's rules differ from BG, **the overlay's rules apply** (Sec. 205-1.2.C). Check the zoning map for the parcel.

## Unincorporated Gwinnett County — C-2 General Business District

### Dimensional standards (Sec. 211-70, Title 2 p. 21–22)

| Standard | C-2 |
|---|---|
| Max FAR | 1.0 |
| Min lot size / width | N/A (larger lots may be required on septic) |
| Max lot coverage | 80% |
| Min front / rear / side setback | 0 / 0 / 0 ft |
| Max building height | 45 ft |

**Zoning buffers (Sec. 230-100, Table 230-2):**
- Rear and side lot lines that abut residential districts need these minimum buffer widths:

| Adjacent district | Buffer |
|---|---|
| Single-family detached | **75 ft** |
| R-TH | 30 ft |
| R-IF | 20 ft |
| Multifamily | 20 ft |
| TND | 40 ft |
| Mixed-use | 20 ft |
| Commercial, O-I or industrial | None |

- All pavement, driveways, dumpsters, parking and retaining walls must be **at least 5 ft from the buffer**.
- Planting must meet the permanent-buffer standards in Title 3.
- Zoning conditions may require more; the stricter rule applies.

**Architecture:** the county's Architectural Design Standards (Sec. 230-110) apply to all buildings in unincorporated Gwinnett.

### Permitted uses (Sec. 211-80 use table, C-2 column)

**Legend:**
- **P** = permitted.
- **S** = Special Use Permit required (Sec. 270-30).
- **C** = conditional use: permitted, subject to the standards in Sec. 211-90.
- **S/C** = Special Use Permit required, and the conditional standards also apply.
- Unlisted uses are prohibited unless the Director classifies them.

**By right (P):**
- **Food and drink:** eating and drinking establishments, **drive-through restaurants**, brewpub, commercial kitchen, catering.
- **Retail:** retail sales and rental, department or wholesale store, furniture, garden supply, home improvement/building supply, copy and print, precious metals dealer, **smoke/novelty/CBD shop**, auto parts store with no installation.
- **Office and medical:** business, medical and professional offices; medical laboratory; radio/TV studio; hospitals; nursing home; personal care homes; continuing-care retirement.
- **Services:** personal services, massage therapy, tattoo and body piercing, locksmith, tutoring, day care center, indoor animal care, drive-up ATM.
- **Recreation:** indoor entertainment and recreation, fitness center, movie theater, pool hall, special events facility.
- **Civic:** civic institutions, community center, clubs and lodges, place of worship.
- **Other:** bed and breakfast, parking lot or structure as a principal use.

**Conditional (C), standards in Sec. 211-90:**
- **Hotels and lodging:** guest rooms must be reached from inside the building, management on duty 24 hours, and no business run from guest rooms.
- **Microbrewery or microdistillery:** may sell only what it produces on site.
- **Other conditional uses:** automobile brokerage office, funeral home without crematory, private or trade school, telecom tower, utility transmission and monitoring facility.
- **Outdoor display or sales of merchandise:** needs a Special Use Permit unless an exception applies.

**Special Use Permit (S or S/C):**
- **Lodging:** extended-stay hotel.
- **Vehicle:** convenience store **with fuel pumps**, **vehicle washing**, vehicle repair and service, vehicle sales, vehicle rental, emissions inspection, parts store with installation.
- **Storage:** **self-storage**; outdoor sales and storage of goods.
- **Nightlife:** lounge or nightclub, hookah/vapor/cigar lounge.
- **Production:** brewery, distillery.
- **Retail and services:** discount/dollar/thrift store, check cashing/payday loan, fireworks store, animal care with outdoor runs, landscaping contractor with outdoor storage.
- **Recreation and lodging:** outdoor entertainment, indoor shooting range, RV park.
- **Institutional:** residential rehabilitation center, residential shelter, cemetery.
- **Other:** ambulance company.

**Not permitted:**
- **Residential:** multifamily dwellings.
- **Retail and services:** pawn shop, bail bonds, appliance/electronics repair, swimming pool sales with outdoor display, taxidermy, driving school.
- **Industrial, offices and storage:** contractors' offices, research and testing labs, data centers, warehouse and distribution, heavy and light industrial.
- **Vehicle:** heavy truck and equipment uses, towing, taxi base.
- **Entertainment:** adult entertainment, movie studio.

### Parking (Sec. 240-20, Table 240-1: minimum and maximum)

| Use | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Sales or rental of goods (freestanding or retail center) | 1 / 500 sf | 1 / 300 sf |
| Office (business, medical, professional) | 1 / 500 sf | 1 / 250 sf |
| Eating and drinking establishment | 1 / 200 sf | 1 / 100 sf |
| Personal services | 1 / 500 sf | 1 / 300 sf |
| Lodging | 0.5 / unit | 1.5 / unit |
| Indoor entertainment or recreation | 1 / 500 sf | 1 / 200 sf |
| Convenience store with fuel pumps | 1 / 500 sf | 1 / 300 sf |
| Day care | 1 / 500 sf | 1 / 300 sf |
| Vehicle repair / service | 1 / 300 sf | 1 / 200 sf |
| Vehicle wash, full-service | 1 / 500 sf + 4 stacking per line | 1 / 300 sf + 10 stacking per line |
| Hospital | 1 / 4 beds | 1 / 2 beds |
| Theater; place of worship | 1 / 4 occupants | 1 / 3 occupants |

**Alternatives (Sec. 240-30):**
- Off-site parking within **500 ft** of the main entrance, with a recorded agreement.
- On-street spaces count at **0.5** each.
- **No maximum** if all parking is in a structure.
- **+10%** over the maximum if the extra parking is on an approved pervious surface.

**Other parking rules:**
- **Space size:** 9 × 18 ft; up to 15% of spaces may be compact.
- **Large lots:** developments over **1,200 spaces** must put at least 50% in a parking structure (Sec. 240-40).
- **Expansions:** additions of **500 sf or more** trigger additional parking.
- **Mixed-use:** mixed-use developments have no minimum.

### Overlays to check (Sec. 214-20)

Gwinnett's corridor overlays near Snellville include:
- **Highway 78 Corridor**
- **Centerville/Highway 124 Corridor**
- **Five Forks Trickum**

These add design requirements for non-residential sites: underground utilities, sidewalks and pedestrian amenity pads, and landscaping, architecture and signage. Whether one applies depends on the parcel; check the county GIS map.

## Caveats and next steps

- **Find the parcel's jurisdiction and zoning first.** Snellville city limits are irregular, and parts of 30078 with a "Snellville" mailing address are in unincorporated Gwinnett. Use the county [GIS Data Browser](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/information-technology-services/geographic-systems/gis-browser), or send me an address and I'll resolve it.
- **Recent zoning conditions** from a rezoning can override any of these numbers. Both jurisdictions routinely attach them, so check the parcel's zoning case history.
- **Snellville's UDO 26-01 amendment** (Chapters 100 and 200) went through hearings in spring 2026. The Municode version used here (online 9-11-2026) is marked modified, so it likely includes UDO 26-01, but confirm with the city if a specific rule is decisive.
- **Not covered:** stream buffers, floodplain, stormwater, signs, tree ordinance, Georgia DOT access permits, and building and fire codes.

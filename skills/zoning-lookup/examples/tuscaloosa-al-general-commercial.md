# Zoning Summary: General Commercial (GC) — City of Tuscaloosa, AL

**Site:** District-level lookup (no specific parcel given)
**Zoning authority:** City of Tuscaloosa (incorporated). Not Tuscaloosa County or the City of Northport, which have separate codes.
**Ordinance:** Code of Ordinances, Chapter 25 – Zoning. Adopted by Ord. No. 9572 on 12-17-2024, effective 1-1-2025. It replaced the 1972 Zoning Ordinance.
**Verified current through:** The cited sections carry amendments through Ord. No. 9789 (7-14-2026). Checked on Municode 2026-11-11.
**Prepared:** 2026-11-11. This is a research summary, not legal advice.

## Bottom line

GC is Tuscaloosa's broad commercial district. It allows most retail, office, restaurant, service, hotel, medical and auto-oriented uses by right. Buildings may be up to **60 ft** tall. Minimum setbacks are **5 ft** on the side and **15 ft** at the rear. The front setback is **20 ft or the average alignment of existing buildings within 200 ft on the same block, whichever is less**. Parking is set **by use**, not by district (for example, retail and office need 3 spaces per 1,000 sf and restaurants need 3.5 per 1,000 sf), and GC has no parking maximum. **There is no maximum lot coverage.** The limits that take its place are a **5% minimum open space** requirement plus setbacks, buffers, landscaping and parking. Two things to watch: housing is allowed **only above a ground floor that is entirely nonresidential**, and **75% of every façade** must be built from the listed primary materials. Near single-family lots, the neighborhood compatibility rules cap buildings at 2 stories within 75 ft and 3 stories within 75–125 ft.

## Permitted uses (GC column, Table V-1 to V-3, accessory & temporary tables)

**Legend (§ 25-98(d)):** P = permitted by right. C = conditional use permit (§ 25-38). S = special exception use permit (§ 25-39). Blank = prohibited. Uses unlisted in the tables are interpreted by the Director of Planning under § 25-103.

**By right (P)**
- **Business services:** office, broadcasting studio, catering, conference/training center, contractor office\*, data center, research & development
- **Food & beverage:** restaurant (quick-service and sit-down), coffee house, brewpub, wine bar, cigar bar
- **Retail & services:** retail sales (small, medium, large\*), convenience store, pharmacy, financial institution, personal services, laundromat, dry-cleaning retail\*, liquor store\*, tattoo\*, tobacco/vape\*, plant nursery\*, funeral home\*, self-service storage\*
- **Lodging & recreation:** hotel\*, indoor and outdoor recreation, theater/auditorium, drive-in theater, private event space\*
- **Vehicle:** automobile wash, light vehicle fuel station\*, light vehicle sales & rental\*, ride-hailing/taxi service
- **Animal care:** kennel\*, pet grooming, veterinary clinic\*
- **Institutional/civic:** medical or dental clinic, vocational school\*, day care center\*, community facility, government facility\*, religious institution, public safety, animal shelter, assisted living, rehabilitation facility, park, greenway, parking facility, bus/rail passenger station, minor utility\*
- **Other:** artisanal production\*
- **Accessory (P):** drive-through facility\*, outdoor seating\*, outdoor storage\*, EV charging station\*, ATM\*, billboard, recycling collection area\*, small-scale solar\*, among others ([§ 25-105](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVUSRE_DIV3ACUSST_S25-105ACUSSTTA))

**Conditional use (C):** bar (club, restaurant, or tavern)\*

**Special exception (S):** live-work dwelling\*, private club/lodge\*, college/university, private and public K-12 schools\*, hospital, hospice, nursing home\*, personal care home\*, light vehicle repair\*, sexually-oriented business\*, vehicle-mounted/tent retail\*, wholesale sales\*. Wireless telecom towers are P or S depending on type\*. The accessory uses home occupation and modular structure are also S.

**Notably prohibited (blank):** stand-alone single-family, townhouse and multifamily dwellings (residential is allowed only above ground-floor nonresidential under § 25-83(d)), student housing, bed & breakfast, RV park, check cashing, casino, heavy vehicle sales/fuel/repair, light and heavy manufacturing, brewery/distillery, warehouse, food processing, truck terminal, major utility, waste-related uses.

\* = use-specific standards apply (see the right-hand column of each table and subsection (c) of §§ 25-100 – 25-102).

## Dimensional standards (Table IV-47, verbatim values)

| | Standard | All uses |
|---|---|---|
| A | Front setback (min) | 20 ft, or the average alignment of existing buildings within 200 ft on either side of subject building and within the same block, whichever is less |
| B | Side setback (min) | 5 ft |
| C | Rear setback (min) | 15 ft |
| D | Building height (max) | 60 ft |

The table has no rows for minimum lot area, lot width, ground coverage ratio or FAR. (The OS district table does use a "ground coverage ratio" row, so its absence from GC is meaningful.)

**Next to single-family lots** ([§ 25-163](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVIDEST_DIV7NECOST_S25-163AP), [§ 25-165](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVIDEST_DIV7NECOST_S25-165NECOST)): these rules apply to new nonresidential and mixed-use development adjacent to, or across a street or alley from, a single-family lot. They also apply to expansions of 50% or more.
- Height is capped at **2 stories within 75 ft** and **3 stories within 75–125 ft** of a single-family lot.
- A pitched roof is required on any part of a building within 75 ft.
- Parking must be at least **10 ft** from single-family lots and screened with a Type C buffer ([§ 25-133](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVIDEST_DIV3LABUST_S25-133BU)).
- Drive-through windows must be at least **100 ft** from single-family zoning, unless they are on the opposite side of the lot. Ordering stations must be at least **150 ft** from single-family lots.

## Parking ([§ 25-123](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVIDEST_DIV2OREPABIPALOST_S25-123OREPAST), Table VI-4; sf = gross floor area)

| Use | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Retail sales; convenience store; pharmacy; financial institution | 3 / 1,000 sf |
| Office; research & development; veterinary clinic | 3 / 1,000 sf |
| Restaurant (quick-service or sit-down); coffee house; bars; wine bar; liquor store | 3.5 / 1,000 sf |
| Medical or dental clinic; personal services; contractor office; tattoo | 2.5 / 1,000 sf |
| Hotel | 1 / guest room + 1 / 250 sf restaurant or meeting area |
| Recreation, indoor | 2 / 1,000 sf |
| Private event space | 4 / 1,000 sf |
| Light vehicle fuel station | 2 / 1,000 sf |
| Light vehicle repair | 4 / 1,000 sf |
| Light vehicle sales & rental | 2.5 / 1,000 sf |
| Automobile wash | 2 stacking or drying spaces / stall |
| Self-service storage | 3 per leasing office |
| Theater/auditorium | 1 / 4 seats |
| Day care center | 2 / 1,000 sf |

- **Calculation:** minimums for multiple uses are added together and rounded up. Unlisted or "variable" uses use one of three methods: the most similar listed use, ITE/ULI/APA references, or a parking demand study (§ 25-123(e)).
- **Maximums:** maximums apply only in the D, DP, DHE and R districts, and to group living (1 space per bedroom), so **GC has no maximum**.
- **Reductions** ([§ 25-124](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVIDEST_DIV2OREPABIPALOST_S25-124OREPAAL)): a parking study can support up to a **60% reduction** in the minimum. Other reduction options can be stacked.
- **EV:** 2% of spaces must be EV spaces at light-vehicle fuel stations and at lots over 50 spaces serving office, hotel or multifamily uses (§ 25-123(d)).
- Bicycle parking (§ 25-126), stacking (§ 25-125) and loading (§ 25-127) have separate standards.

## Lot coverage and open space

- **No maximum lot/building coverage, impervious-surface cap, or FAR** appears in the GC district standards (§ 25-83).
- **Minimum open space:** **5% of total site area** for commercial and mixed-use development in business districts. Multifamily and institutional uses need 7.5% ([§ 25-140, Table VI-13](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVIDEST_DIV4OPSPST_S25-140MIREOPSP)). Required landscape areas count toward it, except perimeter buffers (Table VI-14).
- In practice, coverage is limited by setbacks, required parking, landscaping and buffers ([Art. VI, Div. 3](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVIDEST_DIV3LABUST_S25-128PUIN)), and stormwater rules.

## Other standards that apply (Table IV-48 cross-references)

- **Building materials (§ 25-83(d)(2)):**
  - At least 75% of each façade must be primary materials: brick/tile masonry, natural stone (or a synthetic equivalent), wood clapboard/shingles, glass curtain wall, cementitious siding, or cementitious stucco.
  - Accent materials (up to 25%): pre-cast, EIFS and GFRC for trim only, metal, split-face block, glass block, vinyl.
  - The Director of Planning may approve alternatives.
- **Large retail establishments:** additional form and design standards apply ([§ 25-167](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVIDEST_DIV8FODEST_S25-167LAREES)).
- **Mobility & connectivity:** a circulation plan is required ([§ 25-113 – 25-117](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVIDEST_DIV1MOCOST_S25-113AP)).
- Landscaping and buffers, fences/walls, exterior lighting, green building incentives, **signs** ([Art. VI, Div. 10](https://library.municode.com/al/tuscaloosa/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH25ZO_ARTVIDEST_DIV10SIBI)), and the Subdivision Regulations.
- Existing development: expansions of 50% or more of floor area trigger the mobility and neighborhood compatibility standards "to the maximum extent practicable".

## Caveats and next steps

- **Confirm the parcel is actually zoned GC** on the City's [interactive zoning map](https://tuscaloosa.maps.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=9ea9b83038f342818e8890f4fb3ff897), which shows the map adopted 12-17-2024. Rezonings after that date may not appear on it.
- **Older listings may use old district names.** Pre-2025 listings or surveys may show districts from the old 1972 ordinance (e.g. "BC", "BH", "BN"). Those labels no longer apply, so match the parcel to the current map.
- **The third-party mirror is out of date.** Zoneomics shows an older GC front-setback rule ("average alignment…" with no 20 ft alternative). The official text was amended by Ord. 9751 (3-10-2026). Use Municode.
- **Not covered:** planned development (GPD/RPD) terms, the historic buffer overlay, variances, covenants, building/fire code, stormwater/floodplain, and ALDOT access permits.
